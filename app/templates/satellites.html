1. Find the satellite in the CelesTrak catalogue

CelesTrak is the same catalogue the trajectory calculation reads. Picking the satellite here instead of typing an ID is what keeps the station from tracking the wrong object — a mistaken NORAD ID produces no error, just a wrong pointing.

2. Register the satellite

Only for satellites outside the public catalogue, or to pin a specific TLE while testing. When both lines are filled they take precedence over the NORAD ID, and the elements stop being refreshed automatically.
